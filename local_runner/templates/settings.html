About GNPS Local

GNPS Local is an offline, single-user metabolomics analysis platform that runs the core GNPS (Global Natural Products Social Molecular Networking) workflows entirely on your own machine — no internet connection required after installation.

Supported Workflows
Feature-Based MN (FBMN)
Molecular Community Networking
Tech Stack
